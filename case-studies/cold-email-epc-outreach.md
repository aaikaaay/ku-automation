# Cold Email Sequence — EPC Engineering Leaders
## Case Study: AI-Assisted Engineering Document Review (August 2026)

**Target persona:** Head of Engineering, Discipline Lead, Principal Engineer, or Engineering Manager at Tier-1/Tier-2 EPC contractors and Owner's Engineer / PMC firms.

**Segments to prioritise:**
- Brownfield oil & gas modification specialists
- FEED / detailed design houses with 50–500 engineers
- Owner's Engineer / PMC firms on capex-heavy programmes
- Consulting engineers with EPC delivery arms (Wood, Petrofac tier down)

**Rules:**
- One prospect per company. Senior enough to say yes without a committee.
- Send Tuesday or Wednesday, 08:00–10:00 local time.
- Personalise the opening line — reference a real project, press release, or LinkedIn post from the last 90 days.
- No attachments in email 1. PDF only after a reply.

## Reply-handling scripts

**"Send the case study."**
→ Attach PDF. Add one line: *"Two ways forward — reply with 3 real docs and we'll run a £1,200 supervised pilot, or book 20 minutes here [calendly link] to walk through it."*

**"We already use [tool X]."**
→ *"Understood — most of the tools in that space are unsupervised. The differentiator here is the CEng countersign on every register (professional indemnity attaches). Worth a 20-minute compare on one real deliverable? No obligation."*

**"Not a priority."**
→ *"Fair enough. Can I check back in Q1 2027? I'll send one thing per quarter — a real finding from a live pilot, not a newsletter."*

**"Send to procurement."**
→ *"Happy to. Before I do — is there a specific deliverable class where you'd want a proof point first (pump datasheets / P&IDs / WPS / MTO)? Procurement conversations move faster with one concrete pilot result."*

## Weekly cadence

- **Monday:** research + personalise 3 new prospects
- **Tuesday–Wednesday:** send email 1 to those 3 + follow-ups due
- **Thursday:** LinkedIn DM to any who opened but didn't reply
- **Friday:** log outcomes, update pipeline, decide next week's targets

**Target:** 10 sends per week → 1–2 replies → 1 discovery call → 1 pilot every 4–6 weeks. That's £1,200 in the door + ~30% pilot-to-project-pack conversion = **£2,250 weighted revenue per 10 sends.**

Scale from there.
