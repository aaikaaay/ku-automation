## 📋 POST BODY (copy this verbatim)

> The inspector found the defect at 09:00.
>
> He noted it on his clipboard. Drove 90 minutes to the site office. Typed it up. Sent the NCR.
>
> The rework contractor was notified at 18:30.
>
> In the time between discovery and notification, the joint had been backfilled.
>
> Re-excavation: $47,000. Schedule delay: 11 days.
>
> This is what happens when quality control workflows are built around paper and end-of-day transcription.
>
> On EPC sites — pipelines, offshore platforms, refinery shutdowns, greenfield plants — the gap between observation and corrective action is where budget is lost and schedules slip.
>
> Traditional site inspection looks like this: inspector walks the line, carries clipboard and camera, notes defects manually, drives back to the site office, transcribes notes, attaches photos, generates the NCR, sends it, waits for the contractor to respond.
>
> The observation happened hours ago. The corrective action starts tomorrow. On a fast-track project running on tight float, that lag compounds every day.
>
> Voice agents change the timeline entirely.
>
> An inspector wearing a ruggedised device with an integrated mic walks the weld inspection on section Alpha-12:
>
> "Joint 345. WPS-007 Rev B. Visual inspection: undercut 0.8 millimetres. Exceeds API 1104 limit of 0.5. Non-conformance."
>
> The voice agent captures, structures, and time-stamps the observation in real-time. Pulls the GPS coordinates. Links the photo. Auto-generates an NCR referencing the applicable spec clause. Emails the contractor. Logs the open item in the project management system.
>
> The inspector is already at joint 346.
>
> Time from defect observation to contractor notification: 4 minutes.
>
> Beyond speed, three things change structurally:
>
> 🔴 **Completeness** — The voice agent prompts for every field required by the inspection standard. Paper clipboards don't. Inspectors under time pressure skip fields. Voice agents don't let them.
>
> 🔴 **Safety** — Hands-free operation in confined spaces, at elevation, in full PPE. The inspector's hands are free to hold handrails, operate torches, carry equipment. The report writes itself.
>
> 🔴 **Remote expert access** — A voice-captured observation with structured data and photo can be routed to a specialist in another city in real-time. No travel. Decision in minutes, not days.
>
> On a 500km pipeline project with 40 active inspection zones:
>
> — 400 man-hours of transcription eliminated per day
> — Average corrective-action cycle time: 36 hours → under 4 hours
> — NCR backlog during peak construction: effectively zero
>
> Issues are closed in the same shift they're raised.
>
