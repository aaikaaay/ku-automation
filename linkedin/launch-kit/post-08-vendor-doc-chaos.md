## 📋 POST BODY (copy this verbatim)

> A process engineer on a $280M offshore module project once told me he spent 3 weeks before mechanical completion doing nothing but chasing outstanding vendor documents.
>
> 3 weeks. Not checking quality. Not closing punch items. Chasing documents.
>
> The vendors had submitted them. They were just buried in 11,000 emails across 6 contractors.
>
> This is not an unusual story. In EPC projects, vendor document chaos is so normalised it has its own vocabulary: the VDR goes stale, the SDRL gets missed, the MRB sits half-compiled at handover, and someone's Outlook becomes the de facto document register.
>
> Here's what the chaos actually costs:
>
> 📌 Procurement holds on equipment release — because someone can't confirm the datasheet ITP was approved
> 📌 Construction stoppages — because the installation drawing is still at "Vendor Review" in the register when the steel is already on site
> 📌 Handover delays — because the MRB is missing 14 certificates that nobody tracked properly
> 📌 Warranty disputes — because there's no clean audit trail of what revision was actually installed
>
> The problem is not the vendors. Vendors submit documents. The problem is that the submission, review, comment, and approval cycle runs through email, shared drives, and a spreadsheet register that's 3 weeks behind reality.
>
> What AI fixes here:
>
> ✅ **Automated VDR reconciliation** — AI ingests transmittal logs, email attachments, and EDMS folders and builds the real-time status of every document against the SDRL. No manual updates. No "who has the master copy" conversations.
>
> ✅ **AI-powered technical review** — instead of a senior process engineer spending 4 hours checking a heat exchanger datasheet line-by-line against the datasheet spec and TEMA standard, the AI does the first pass in 8 minutes: flags every non-conformance, cites the clause, and generates a structured comment register ready to transmit.
>
> ✅ **Smart comment closeout tracking** — AI monitors vendor responses against open comments and flags unresolved items before they get buried in the next revision cycle. Nothing falls through.
>
> ✅ **MRB compilation support** — AI maps every required certificate, test record, and datasheet to the equipment tag list and identifies gaps 8 weeks before handover — not the night before.
>
> The senior engineer's job becomes approving the AI's findings, not generating them.
>
