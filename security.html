Data Handling & Retention

Precise rules. Automatic enforcement.

Vague "we keep your data safe" promises aren't good enough for engineering documents. Here is exactly what happens to your files.

Encrypted in Transit

All uploads and downloads are encrypted using TLS 1.3. Older TLS versions are rejected at the load balancer. HSTS enforced.

Encrypted at Rest

Files stored on disk are encrypted with AES-256. Keys are rotated on a defined schedule and are never co-located with the encrypted data.

30-Day Auto-Purge

Source documents and review outputs are automatically deleted from primary storage 30 days after delivery. No exceptions, no accumulation.

On-Demand Purge

Can't wait 30 days? Request immediate deletion via the portal or by emailing contact@ku-automation.com. Target SLA: 24 hours.

Backups ≤ 7 Days

Encrypted cold-storage backups are retained for a maximum of 7 days, then permanently deleted. Backups are AES-256 encrypted at rest.

Controlled Data Boundary

Client data does not leave KU Automation's controlled infrastructure except for the specific AI model API calls described in the next section. No marketing tools, analytics platforms, or CRM systems receive your document content.

Access Control

Who sees your documents? Exactly one person.

Access to client deliverables is deliberately narrow. Here's the full access matrix.

Multi-Tenant Isolation

Every client's reviews are logically segregated in the database using row-level security in PostgreSQL. A session token for Client A cannot query or access records belonging to Client B — enforced at the database layer, not just application logic.

Admin Access: One Person

Only Kingsley Uzowulu (CEng MIMechE) holds admin-level access to client review data. There are no offshore development teams, no shared ops accounts, no sub-contracted administrators with access to client deliverables.

Sub-Processor Scope Limits

Stripe processes payment and sees only invoice metadata (amount, currency, timestamp) — never your review content, filenames, or project details. No sub-processor has direct access to engineering deliverables.

Session Security & MFA

Portal sessions use JWT-based authentication with short token expiry. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is configurable on the enterprise tier. All session activity is logged with IP and timestamp.

Infrastructure

Built to run without surprises.

The platform is hardened, monitored, and patched continuously. Incidents are surfaced to clients fast — we don't sit on breach notifications.

Hardened Node.js Runtime

Portal backend runs on hardened Node.js infrastructure with continuous uptime monitoring and anomaly alerting. Production environment is separate from development; no staging credentials in production.

Dependency Vulnerability Scanning

All npm dependencies are scanned automatically (Dependabot + npm audit). Critical and high CVEs are patched within 48 hours of disclosure. Dependency lock files are committed and reviewed on every merge.

Automated Encrypted Backups

Database and file-store backups run automatically on a defined schedule. All backup snapshots are AES-256 encrypted. Restore procedures are tested periodically.

Incident Response: 4-Hour P1 Notification

For any P1 incident (confirmed data exposure or service breach), affected clients are notified within 4 hours of detection. We do not wait for legal counsel to clear a notification — you hear from us first.

What We Ask From You

A few reasonable requests.

Security is a two-way responsibility. Here's what we need from you to keep engagements safe and compliant.

Redact Sensitive Numbers

If your document contains final contract prices, bid margins, or commercially sensitive figures that are not relevant to the engineering review, redact them before uploading. The review doesn't need them.

Upload Only Authorised Documents

Never upload documents you are not contractually or legally authorised to share with a third-party engineering reviewer. This includes NDA-restricted client deliverables you have received — unless your NDA with that party explicitly permits this use.

Notify Us of Special Controls

If any document is subject to special handling controls — ITAR, EAR, UK MoD restricted, government-classified, or equivalent — notify us before upload at contact@ku-automation.com. We may need to decline the engagement or establish specific safeguards.
